# Preparing for Shabbat — Cooper Family Edition

*Official Family Rulebook · 1–4 Players · Ages 6+ · 15–20 Minutes*

Components: 72 poker-size cards + 4 3D-Printed Family Helpers (Abba, Nael, Mommy, Mekimi) | Play Online: https://joshcooper417.github.io/Shabbat-game/

---

## 1. Welcome, Goal & Players

**You may think you are just coming to play a game, but today we are Mommy, Abba, Nael, and Mekimi!**

It's Friday afternoon in Givot Eden—challah is rising, toys are spilling out of the Mamad, and winter sunset is coming fast! Dash through the Cooper house together to finish every **Mission** before **Shabbat Begins!** flips face-up on the **Shabbat Clock**.

> **Win Right Away:** The instant your family finishes the final Mission, **Shabbat Shalom—the game ends immediately and you win!**
> **Lose Right Away:** If **Shabbat Begins!** flips face-up before all Missions are done, stop right away and try again!

**Table Talk:** This is a collaborative game so discuss freely! The player taking the current turn makes the final call.

> **Clockwise Turn Order (Every Game):** 1. Abba → 2. Nael → 3. Mommy → 4. Mekimi

### Choosing Helpers (1–4 Players)

All 4 Helpers always play in every game. Pick whoever you want—let the youngest player pick first, choose freely, or deal them randomly:

- **4 Players:** Each player controls 1 Helper.
- **3 Players:** One player controls 2 Helpers (for example, Abba & Mekimi); the other two control 1 each.
- **2 Players:** Player 1 controls **Abba & Mommy** (turns 1 & 3); Player 2 controls **Nael & Mekimi** (turns 2 & 4) to alternate every turn!
- **1 Player (Solo):** Control all 4 Helpers in order.

## 3. Setup (Standard Mode)

*First game? Check Section 9 for Beginner Mode! See the Table Layout diagram below as you set up.*

1. **Build the 3×3 House:** Every Friday rush hits different corners of the house—shuffle the 12 Room cards and deal **9** bright-side upright (**Clear**) in a 3×3 grid with about 1 inch of space between cards so any room can turn sideways cleanly (the other 3 spots are already taken care of in the box!). Make sure all 4 House Zones appear—if a Zone is missing, swap the **bottom-right room** for a **random** set-aside room of that Zone, then box the 3 unused rooms. *(Rooms from the same Zone don't need to touch—walk straight card-to-card across the grid as you race around the house!)*
2. **Starting Clutter:** Turn the **Top-Center** and **Bottom-Center** rooms sideways (**Cluttered**). Leave all other **7 rooms** (the Center, both sides, and all 4 corners) upright (**Clear**).
3. **Place the 4 Helpers:** Set the 3D Helpers clockwise in the 4 corners: **Top-Left:** Abba · **Top-Right:** Nael · **Bottom-Right:** Mommy · **Bottom-Left:** Mekimi. Place each Helper Power Card face-up in front of the player who chose that Helper (Section 1).
4. **Deal the Missions:** Shuffle all 18 Mission cards and deal **13** (box the other 5 unseen). Flip **4 face-up** into the **Mission Row** and stack **9 face-down** as the **Mission Deck**. Leave space for the **Done Pile**.
5. **Build the Shabbat Clock:** Place **5 Friday Afternoon** cards face-down left to right, with **Shabbat Begins!** face-down on the far right (6 total; box the other 4).
6. **Tools & Event Deck:** Shuffle all 10 Tools face-down: place **2 face-up** in the **Family Toolbox**, box **4 unseen**, and shuffle the **remaining 4** with all **18 Slow-Downs** face-down to form the **Event Deck** (22 cards). Leave space for an **Event Discard Pile**.

## 4. Key Terms & How a Turn Works

### Key Terms

- **3D Helpers vs. Helper Cards:** Your 4 3D-Printed Helpers stand and move on top of the 3×3 Room cards. Your 4 Helper Power Cards stay face-up in front of their players to show powers and hold Errands you are carrying.
- **Room States:** Every Room card is **Clear** (bright side upright), **Cluttered** (bright side turned sideways), or **Blocked** (flipped upright to its red-striped back—the Zone badge stays visible). Helpers standing on a room stay there when it turns sideways or flips Blocked.
- **Next-Door Room:** Straight up, down, left, or right (**not diagonal**).
- **Matching a Card to a Room:** Each of the 4 House Zones always uses the same color and icon together. When a Mission or Event shows a Zone badge, it matches **any** Room card in that Zone (for example, any orange Kitchen card works on any Kitchen room).
- **Clock Flip:** Flip the leftmost face-down Shabbat Clock card face-up.

### On Every Turn: Just 2 Easy Steps!

- **Step 1 — Take Up to 2 Actions** (any order; repeats allowed! Free bonuses may happen before, between, or after your 2 Actions.): Choose from the **4 Actions** in Section 5: **Move**, **Tidy Room**, **Do a Mission**, or **Use a Household Tool**.
- **Step 2 — Flip 1 Event Card** (Section 6): Flip and resolve the top card of the Event Deck, then pass play clockwise (**Abba → Nael → Mommy → Mekimi**). **After Mekimi's turn**, do 1 **Clock Flip** and start the next round with **Abba**!

## 5. The 4 Actions

On your turn, spend up to **2 Actions** on any mix of these **4 Actions**—you may repeat the same action twice (like Move → Move), or stop early:

1. **Move** — Walk 1 next-door room
2. **Tidy Room** — Clear or Unblock
3. **Do a Mission** — Team Chore or Errand
4. **Use a Tool** — Play from Toolbox

> **Family Superpowers:** Each Cooper Helper bends one of the core rules—see Section 7 once you've read all 4 Actions and Events!

### Action 1: Move (1 Action)

- Walk your 3D Helper **1 room** straight up, down, left, or right (**not diagonal**).
- You may walk into and out of Clear, Cluttered, and Blocked rooms.

### Action 2: Tidy Room (1 Action)

- **Tidy Cluttered → Clear:** Stand in a sideways room and turn it upright (1 Helper).
- **Unblock Red Blocked → Clear:** Flip a red Blocked room straight back to Clear! Requires **2 or more Helpers** standing in that room.

### Action 3: Do a Mission (1 Action)

There are **2 types of Missions**—each card shows the House Zone(s) where you do it:

- **Team Chores (10 cards · TEAM CHORE · 2+):** With **2 or more Helpers** standing together in a **Clear** room of the matching Zone, spend **1 Action** to move the card straight to the **Done Pile**!
- **Errands (8 cards · ERRAND · Start → End):**
  - **Pick Up (1 Action):** In a **Clear** room matching the **Start** Zone, place the Errand on your Helper Power Card *(no carry limit!)*.
  - **Deliver (1 Action):** In a **Clear** room matching the **End** Zone, move 1 carried Errand to the **Done Pile**!

> **Free Handoff (Free Bonus — Any Room!):** Anytime during Step 1 on your turn, you may freely give or take any carried Errand(s) with a teammate standing in your room at no Action cost.
>
> **Instant Refill & Win Check:** Whenever a Mission leaves the **Mission Row** (finished or picked up), immediately flip the top card of the **Mission Deck** (while cards remain) so 4 stay face-up. The instant the final Mission reaches the **Done Pile** (Row, Deck, and all Helper cards empty), you win!

### Action 4: Use a Household Tool (1 Action — Any Room!)

Spend **1 Action from any room** to play a face-up Tool from the **Family Toolbox**:

- **Ice Coffee Boost (4 cards · Reusable):** Turn **1 or 2 Cluttered rooms** anywhere on the board back to Clear *(cannot fix Blocked rooms)*, then place this card in the **Event Discard Pile**.
- **Strategic Snack Summons (3 cards · Reusable):** Pull **any 1 teammate** from anywhere on the board straight into your room *(their carried Errands come with them!)*, then place this card in the **Event Discard Pile**.
- **Cousin Aviel Drops By! (3 cards · One-Time Shield):** Place face-up on the leftmost unshielded face-down Clock card. **The next time that Clock card would flip for any reason, return Cousin Aviel to the box instead!** *(Multiple shields may guard consecutive Clock cards.)*

> **Great 2-Action Combos:** Tidy a Cluttered room on Action 1 and immediately finish a Mission there on Action 2—or play **Snack Summons** on Action 1 and finish a **Team Chore** together on Action 2!

## 6. Events & End of Round

After your 2 Actions, flip the top card of the **Event Deck**. If the deck is empty, shuffle the entire **Event Discard Pile** face-down first—so spent Reusable Tools naturally return to the deck!

- **Household Tool:** Place it face-up in the **Family Toolbox** (no limit!).
- **Slow-Down:** Scan rooms in the matching Zone **left to right, top to bottom** (skip Blocked rooms):
  1. If any matching room is **Cluttered**, flip the **1st Cluttered** room to **Red Blocked**!
  2. Else if any matching room is **Clear**, turn the **1st Clear** room sideways (**Cluttered**)!
  3. If all matching rooms are already Blocked, nothing happens.
  Then place the Slow-Down in the **Event Discard Pile**.
- **Double Trouble (Double Slow-Down!):** Triggers **two Slow-Downs on one card**—resolve a normal Slow-Down for the **left** Zone badge first, then a second Slow-Down for the **right** Zone badge. Discard to the **Event Discard Pile**.
- **Time Flies (*“Wait—Shabbat Is at WHAT Time?!”*):** (1) Do **1 Clock Flip** right away (*Shabbat Begins!* = lose unless **Cousin Aviel** shields it); (2) **resolve a Kitchen Slow-Down** (flip the 1st Cluttered Kitchen Blocked, else turn the 1st Clear Kitchen sideways); (3) **return this card to the box**.

> **End of Round (Only After Mekimi's Turn!):** Do 1 **Clock Flip** (or box a Cousin Aviel shield on it). If *Shabbat Begins!* flips, you lose! Otherwise, start the next round with **Abba**.

## 7. Meet the 4 Cooper Helpers

Every Helper gets **2 Actions per turn** plus their own Cooper family superpower:

| Helper | Corner | Superpower |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. ABBA** | Top-Left | **Solo Cleanup (Your Turn):** Unblock a Red Blocked room alone (1 Action). **Clutter Guard (Always On):** While Abba stands in a Cluttered room, Slow-Downs can't flip it Blocked (only that Zone's Slow-Down is canceled—the other Zone on Double Trouble still happens!). |
| **2. NAEL** | Top-Right | **Learning Tower Power! (Always On):** While Nael and a teammate share a **Cluttered** room, either one can finish a **Team Chore** there on either player's turn (not Blocked). |
| **3. MOMMY** | Bottom-Right | **Errand Express (Your Turn):** Once per turn, Pick Up or Deliver an Errand for **FREE** (before, between, or after your 2 Actions!). Pick Up & Deliver also work in **Cluttered** rooms (not Blocked)! |
| **4. MEKIMI** | Bottom-Left | **Turbo Crawl (Your Turn):** Whenever Mekimi spends 1 Action to Move, crawl straight into **ANY room** on the board! |

## 8. Room States: CAN & CANNOT

Check the orientation of the Room card your Helper stands on:

| Room State | ✓ You CAN | ✗ You CANNOT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clear (upright)** | ✓ Everything! Move in/out, Team Chores, Pick Up/Deliver/Handoff Errands, and Tools. | Nothing—everything works here! |
| **Cluttered (sideways)** | ✓ Move in/out, Tidy (1 Helper), Free Handoff, Tools. Team Chores if Nael + teammate are here. Errands on Mommy's turn. | ✗ Team Chores (without Nael). ✗ Pick Up or Deliver Errands (if not Mommy). |
| **Blocked (red back)** | ✓ Move in/out, Unblock (2+ Helpers or Abba alone), Free Handoff, Tools. | ✗ Team Chores. ✗ Pick Up or Deliver Errands (not even Nael or Mommy!). |

> **Watch Out for Cluttered Rooms!** Slow-Downs always flip Cluttered rooms to **Red Blocked** first—Tidy messy rooms early (or stand **Abba** there with *Clutter Guard*)!

## 9. Difficulty Modes

All modes use **9 Rooms** (3 boxed), **4 Helpers**, and **6 Tools** (**2** in Family Toolbox + **4** in Event Deck; 4 boxed). Always keep **4 Missions** face-up in the Mission Row.

| Mode | Missions (Row+Deck / Box) | Shabbat Clock (Aft+Shab / Box) | Starting Clutter | Slow-Downs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Beginner (First Game · 6+)** | 10 (4+6 / 8 boxed) | 7 (6+1 / 3 boxed) | 1 room (Center) | 17 (Box Time Flies!) |
| **Standard (Family Balance)** | 13 (4+9 / 5 boxed) | 6 (5+1 / 4 boxed) | 2 rooms (Top & Bot Center) | 18 (All included) |
| **Hard (Friday Rush)** | 15 (4+11 / 3 boxed) | 6 (5+1 / 4 boxed) | 2 rooms (Top & Bot Center) | 18 (All included) |

> **Family Tip:** Play **Beginner** once so kids learn the flow (almost every family wins their first game!), then step up to **Standard** for thrilling final-round finishes—and try **Hard** when your family is ready for a true Friday afternoon sprint!

## 10. Quick 1-Round Example

A sample Standard-mode round showing combos and all 4 Cooper Helpers:

- **1. Abba (2 Actions):** Walks right into Red Blocked *The Salon* (Action 1) and uses **Solo Cleanup** to flip it Clear alone (Action 2)! *Event:* Kitchen Slow-Down → Cluttered *The Fridge* (where Mommy stands) flips Blocked.
- **2. Nael (2 Actions):** Plays **Snack Summons** to pull Abba into Nael's Cluttered *Dining Table* (Action 1), then uses **Learning Tower Power!** to finish *Set the Shabbat Table* right away (Action 2)! Refill Mission Row to 4. *Event:* Draws *Cousin Aviel Drops By!* → added to Family Toolbox!
- **3. Mommy (2 Actions + Free Errand):** Walks out of Blocked *Fridge* into Clear *Chairs* (Action 1), Delivers *Challah* for **FREE** (**Errand Express**!), and puts *Cousin Aviel* on the Clock (Action 2)! *Event:* Downstairs Slow-Down → Clear *Salon* tilts sideways.
- **4. Mekimi (2 Actions):** Uses **Turbo Crawl** straight to Clear *Giant Island* (Action 1) and Picks Up *Showstopper* Errand (Action 2)! Refill Mission Row. *Event:* Table Slow-Down targets Cluttered *Dining Table*—**Abba's Clutter Guard** cancels the Slow-Down! *End of Round:* **Cousin Aviel** blocks the Clock Flip and returns to the box! The next round starts with **Abba**!

## Kid's First-Game Guide (6+)

> **How We Win:** Finish **all the Missions** together before **Shabbat Begins!** flips over!

### On Your Turn: 2 Actions + 1 Event!

1. **Pick Any 2 Fun Actions** (you can even do the same one twice!):
   - **Walk** 1 room up, down, left, or right. *(Mekimi crawls ANYWHERE!)*
   - **Tidy** a sideways room (alone) or a Red room (with a friend—or Abba alone!).
   - **Team Chore (2+):** Meet a friend in a Clear room of that Zone and finish it!
   - **Errand (Start → End):** Pick up in a Clear Start room, carry it, and drop off in a Clear End room—pass it to a friend in your room anytime for free!
   - **Use a Tool** from the Toolbox (from any room)!
2. **Flip 1 Event Card:** Tool → Toolbox! Slow-Down → find rooms in that Zone: flip the 1st sideways room Red (or tilt the 1st Clear room sideways!).
3. **After Mekimi:** Flip 1 Clock card closer to sunset, then go back to **Abba**!

> **Superpowers:** **Abba** fixes Red rooms alone & keeps his sideways room from flipping Red · **Nael** & a friend finish Team Chores in sideways rooms · **Mommy** gets 1 FREE Pick Up or Deliver/turn (even sideways!) · **Mekimi** crawls ANYWHERE!

## Tabletop Quick Reference

> **Turn Order:** Abba → Nael → Mommy → Mekimi → **Clock Flip!** | **Every Turn:** **2 Actions** (+ Free Handoff) → **Flip 1 Event**

- **4 Actions (Pick Any 2):** **Move** (1 room straight) · **Tidy** (Cluttered: 1 / Red: 2+ or Abba) · **Mission** (Team Chore 2+ or Errand) · **Tool** (Play from Toolbox).
- **Slow-Downs:** Scan matching Zone L→R, T→B: **1st Cluttered → Blocked!** Else **1st Clear → Cluttered!**
- **Room States Cheat Sheet (Keep Face-Up on Table!):**
  - **Clear (Upright):** Everything works! Team Chores, Errands, Handoffs, Tools.
  - **Cluttered (Sideways):** Walk, Tidy (1), Handoff, Tools. *(Team w/ Nael; Errands w/ Mommy.)*
  - **Red Blocked:** Walk, Handoff, Tools only. Unblock w/ **2+ Helpers** or **Abba**!
- **4 Family Superpowers:**
  - **Abba:** Unblocks Red alone; his Cluttered room can't flip Red (canceled!).
  - **Nael:** With teammate in Cluttered room, either finishes Team Chores (either's turn).
  - **Mommy:** 1 FREE Pick Up or Deliver/turn (plus 2 Actions!); works in Cluttered.
  - **Mekimi:** Crawls straight into **ANY room** on any Move!
- **Modes:** Beg **10** Missions / **7** Clock (no Time Flies) · Std **13** / **6** · Hard **15** / **6**
