# Preparing for Shabbat — Cooper Family Edition (Official Family Rulebook · v3 Simplified)

**Players:** 1–4 (cooperative, ages 6+) · **Time:** ~15–20 minutes · **Components:** 72 poker-size cards + 4 3D-Printed Family Helpers (**Abba, Mommy, Nael, and Mekimi**)

*(First time playing? Use **Beginner Mode** in Section 7, and hand kids the **1-Page Kid's Guide** in Section 10!)*

---

## 1. Goal of the Game

Work together as a family to finish all **Game Chores** before **Shabbat Begins!** flips face-up on the **Shabbat Clock**.

- **Win Right Away:** The instant the final Chore reaches the **Done Pile** (meaning the Chore Row, Chore Deck, and all carried Errands are empty), **Shabbat Shalom — you win immediately!** Skip the Event flip and any Clock flip.
- **Lose Right Away:** If **Shabbat Begins!** flips face-up on the Clock before all Chores are done, Shabbat has begun before the house is ready — stop right away and try again!

### Bird's-Eye Table Layout

Set up your table like this before Turn 1:

```text
  SHABBAT CLOCK (left → right, face-down; Shabbat Begins! on far right)
  [ Fri Aft ] [ Fri Aft ] [ Fri Aft ] [ Fri Aft ] [ Fri Aft ] [ SHABBAT BEGINS! ]

  CHORES (4 face-up + Deck + Done)          THE 3×3 HOUSE (9 Room Cards)
  +---------+---------+                     +-----------+-----------+-----------+
  | Chore 1 | Chore 2 |                     |  ABBA     | Cluttered |   NAEL    |
  +---------+---------+                     | (Top-Left)| (Sideways)| (Top-Rt)  |
  | Chore 3 | Chore 4 |                     +-----------+-----------+-----------+
  +---------+---------+                     |  Clear    |   Clear   |   Clear   |
  [Chore Deck] [Done Pile]                  +-----------+-----------+-----------+
                                            |  MEKIMI   | Cluttered |   MOMMY   |
  EVENTS & FAMILY TOOLBOX                   | (Bot-Left)| (Sideways)| (Bot-Rt)  |
  [Event Deck] [Event Discard]              +-----------+-----------+-----------+
  Toolbox (face-up): [Tool 1] [Tool 2]
                                            HELPER POWER CARDS (face-up by players)
                                            [ Abba ] [ Nael ] [ Mommy ] [ Mekimi ]
```

### Key Basics

- **3D Helpers vs. Helper Cards:** Your **4 3D-Printed Family Helpers** move around on top of the 3×3 Room cards. Your **4 Helper Power Cards** stay face-up in front of the players as a reminder of each Helper's superpower and to hold any **Errands** they are carrying. *(Any number of Helpers may share the same room!)*
- **Room States:** Every Room card is **Clear** (upright), **Cluttered** (turned sideways), or **Blocked** (flipped over to its shaded red side — the zone icon stays visible on the red back so nothing is hidden).
- **Next-Door Room:** A room straight **up, down, left, or right** of your room (**not diagonal**).
- **Matching Room:** Match the color and icon! Any orange **Kitchen** Chore matches any orange **Kitchen** room; teal **Downstairs** matches Downstairs; blue **Table** matches Table; purple **Upstairs** matches Upstairs.
- **Clock Flip:** Flipping the leftmost face-down Clock card face-up.
- **Table Talk:** Talk, cheer, and strategize freely! On each turn, the player controlling the active Helper makes the final call.

## 2. What's in the Box (72 Cards + 4 3D-Printed Family Helpers)

1. **Rooms & Helpers (16 cards):**
   - **12 Double-Sided Room Cards** (3 each of **Kitchen**, **Downstairs Balagan**, **Table**, and **Upstairs**) — Clear/Cluttered front, shaded red **Blocked** back.
   - **4 Helper Power Cards** matching your **4 3D-Printed Family Helpers**: **Abba**, **Mommy**, **Nael**, and **Mekimi**.
2. **Chores Deck (18 cards):**
   - **10 Team Chores:** Finish together with **2 or more Helpers** in a matching Clear room.
   - **8 Errands:** Shows a **Start zone → End zone**. Pick it up in a matching Clear Start room, carry it on your Helper card, and deliver it to a matching Clear End room.
3. **Shabbat Clock Deck (10 cards):**
   - **9 Friday Afternoon** cards + **1 Shabbat Begins!** card.
4. **Friday Events & Tools Deck (28 cards, shared back):**
   - **18 Slow-Downs:** 15 Single-Zone cards, 2 Double Trouble cards (*Challah Flour Trail!* Kitchen + Downstairs; *Table-to-Upstairs Whirlwind!* Table + Upstairs), and 1 Time Flies card (*"Wait—Shabbat Is at WHAT Time?!"* — Clock flip + Kitchen).
   - **10 Household Tools:** 4 *Ice Coffee Boost*, 3 *Strategic Snack Summons*, and 3 *Cousin Aviel Drops By!*.

## 3. Setup (Standard Mode — See Section 7 for Beginner & Hard)

1. **Build the 3×3 House:** Shuffle the 12 Room cards and deal **9** face-up and **Clear** (upright) in a 3×3 grid, leaving a small gap between cards so they can turn sideways. Make sure all 4 zone colors/icons appear — if one is missing, swap the **bottom-right room** for a boxed room of the missing zone. Put the 3 unused rooms in the box.
2. **Starting Clutter:** Turn the **Top-Center** and **Bottom-Center** Room cards **sideways (Cluttered)**. *(All 4 corner rooms start Clear!)*
3. **Place the 4 Helpers:** Set the 4 3D-Printed Family Helpers in the 4 corners:
   - **Top-Left:** Abba · **Top-Right:** Nael · **Bottom-Left:** Mekimi · **Bottom-Right:** Mommy.
   Place the 4 Helper Power Cards face-up near the players.
4. **Deal the Chores:** Shuffle all 18 Chore cards once and deal **13** for this game (box the other 5 unseen). Flip **4 face-up** to form the **Chore Row**, and stack the remaining **9 face-down** as the **Chore Deck**. Leave room for a **Done Pile**.
5. **Build the Shabbat Clock:** Place **5 Friday Afternoon** cards face-down in a row left to right, with **Shabbat Begins!** face-down at the **far right** (6 Clock cards total; box the 4 unused Friday Afternoon cards).
6. **Set Up Tools & the Event Deck:**
   - Shuffle all 10 Tools face-down: place **2 face-up** in the **Family Toolbox** (no size limit), shuffle **4** together with **all 18 Slow-Downs** face-down to form the **Event Deck** (22 cards), and box the remaining 4 Tools unseen.
   - Leave space next to the Event Deck for **one single face-up Event Discard Pile**.

## 4. Playing with 1–4 Players & Turn Order

Every round plays in the exact same clockwise order:

> **Abba → Nael → Mommy → Mekimi**

- **4 Players:** Each player controls 1 Helper.
- **3 Players:** The oldest player (or any volunteer) controls **2 Helpers** (for example, Abba and Mekimi); the other two players control 1 Helper each.
- **2 Players:** Player 1 controls **Abba & Mommy** (turns 1 & 3); Player 2 controls **Nael & Mekimi** (turns 2 & 4) so you naturally alternate turns!
- **1 Player (Solo):** Control all 4 Helpers in order.

*(There is no Ready/Resting card flipping! After Mekimi finishes her turn, the round ends — flip 1 Clock card and start the next round with Abba.)*

## 5. How to Play a Turn

On your Helper's turn, do **2 steps in order**:

### Step 1: Take Up to 2 Actions *(any order, repeats allowed!)*

Pick any **2 Actions** from the 4 choices below (you may repeat the same action twice, or stop early if you want):

1. **Move (1 Action):** Walk **1 room** straight up, down, left, or right (**not diagonal**).
   - *Cluttered and Blocked rooms never trap you — you can always enter or leave them normally.*
   - *Any number of Helpers may share a room.*
   - *(**Mekimi's Turbo Crawl:** Whenever Mekimi spends 1 Action to Move, she may crawl straight into **ANY room** on the board!)*
2. **Tidy Room (1 Action):**
   - **Cluttered → Clear:** Turn your sideways room upright (1 Helper).
   - **Blocked → Clear:** Flip your red Blocked room straight back to Clear. Requires **2 or more Helpers** in the room — or **Abba alone** (*Solo Cleanup*).
3. **Do a Chore (1 Action — matching Clear room):**
   - **Team Chore:** With at least **1 teammate** in your matching Clear room *(or Cluttered if **Nael** is here!)*, move 1 matching Team Chore from the Chore Row to the **Done Pile**.
   - **Pick Up an Errand:** In a matching Clear Start room *(or Cluttered on **Mommy's** turn!)*, place 1 matching Errand from the Chore Row onto your Helper card. *(No carry limit!)*
   - **Deliver an Errand:** In a matching Clear End room *(or Cluttered on **Mommy's** turn!)*, move 1 carried Errand from your Helper card to the **Done Pile**.
   - **Instant Refill:** Whenever a Chore leaves the Chore Row (finished or picked up), **immediately flip the top card of the Chore Deck** so **4 Chores** stay face-up (until the Chore Deck runs out).
   - **Free Handoff (0 Actions — Anytime on Your Turn, Any Room):** Give or take any carried Errand(s) with a teammate standing in your room! *(Does not cost an Action and works even in Cluttered or Blocked rooms. Carried Errands cannot be dropped back into the Chore Row.)*
4. **Use a Household Tool (1 Action — From Any Room!):** Spend 1 face-up Tool from the **Family Toolbox**:
   - **Ice Coffee Boost (Reusable):** Turn **1 or 2 Cluttered rooms** anywhere on the board back to Clear *(cannot fix Blocked rooms)*, then put this card in the **Event Discard Pile**.
   - **Strategic Snack Summons (Reusable):** Pull **any 1 teammate** from anywhere on the board straight into your room *(carried Errands travel with them!)*, then put this card in the **Event Discard Pile**.
   - **Cousin Aviel Drops By! (One-Time Shield):** Place face-up on top of the leftmost unshielded face-down Clock card. The next time that Clock card would flip (from Round End or *Time Flies*), return *Cousin Aviel* to the box instead! *(Multiple Cousin Aviel cards may guard separate Clock cards in a row.)*

### Room States: What You CAN & CANNOT Do

| Room State | You CAN | You CANNOT | How to Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clear (upright)** | Move in/out, finish **Team Chores**, **Pick Up / Deliver / Handoff Errands**, and use **Tools**. | Nothing — everything works here! | Already Clear! |
| **Cluttered (sideways)** | Move in/out, **Tidy**, **Free Handoff**, and use **Tools**. Also: **Team Chores** work if **Nael + a teammate** are here; **Pick Up / Deliver Errands** work on **Mommy's turn**. | Finish Team Chores *(without Nael)* or Pick Up/Deliver Errands *(if not Mommy)*. | **Tidy** (1 Helper) or **Ice Coffee Boost**. |
| **Blocked (red side)** | Move in/out, **Unblock** (2+ Helpers or Abba alone), **Free Handoff**, and use **Tools**. | Do **any** Team Chores or Pick Up / Deliver Errands *(not even with Nael or Mommy!)*. | **Tidy** with **2+ Helpers** or **Abba alone** *(Ice Coffee cannot fix Blocked)*. |

### Step 2: Flip 1 Event Card *(Skip if You Already Won!)*

Flip the top card of the **Event Deck**:

- **If the Event Deck is empty:** Shuffle the entire **Event Discard Pile** (both Slow-Downs and spent Reusable Tools!) face-down to make a fresh Event Deck, then flip 1 card.
- **Household Tool:** Good news! Place it face-up in the **Family Toolbox** (no limit).
- **Slow-Down (Universal Cluttered-First Rule):**
  - Scan rooms matching the card's zone icon **left to right, top to bottom** (skipping rooms that are already **Blocked**):
    1. If **any** matching room is **Cluttered**, flip the **1st Cluttered** room over to its red **Blocked** side! *(Exception: while **Abba** stands in a Cluttered room, Slow-Downs can't flip it Blocked — the hit is canceled!)*
    2. Otherwise, if **any** matching room is **Clear**, turn the **1st Clear** room sideways (**Cluttered**)!
    3. If **all** rooms of that zone are already Blocked, nothing happens.
  - Discard the Slow-Down face-up into the **Event Discard Pile**.
  - **Double Trouble:** Resolve the left zone icon first, then the right zone icon using the exact same rule!
  - **Time Flies (*"Wait—Shabbat Is at WHAT Time?!"*):**
    1. Trigger **1 Clock Flip** first (if *Cousin Aviel* is guarding that Clock card, box *Cousin Aviel* instead; if **Shabbat Begins!** flips, you lose immediately!).
    2. Then apply a **Kitchen** Slow-Down hit (1st Cluttered Kitchen → Blocked; else 1st Clear Kitchen → Cluttered).
    3. **Always return this card to the box** (never put it in Event Discard).

### End of Round (Only After Mekimi's Turn!)

After **Mekimi** finishes her Event flip, the round ends:
1. Trigger **1 Clock Flip** on the leftmost face-down Clock card (or box a *Cousin Aviel* shield sitting on it).
2. If **Shabbat Begins!** flips face-up, the game ends right away! Otherwise, start the next round with **Abba**.

## 8. Quick 1-Round Example (Standard Mode)

- **Board Start (Round 2):** Abba is in *The Giant Island* (Top-Left, Kitchen, Clear). Top-Center is *The Salon* (Downstairs, Blocked!). Nael is in *The Dining Table* (Top-Right, Table, Cluttered). Mommy is in *The Fridge* (Bottom-Right, Kitchen, Cluttered) carrying *Challah: Mixer to Oven* (Kitchen → Table). Mekimi is in *The Bathtub* (Bottom-Left, Upstairs, Clear).
- **Chore Row:** *Set the Shabbat Table* (Table Team Chore), *Half Baked Harvest Showstopper* (Kitchen → Table Errand), *Bath Time Splash Squad* (Upstairs Team Chore), *Mamad Toy Avalanche* (Downstairs Team Chore).
- **Family Toolbox:** *Strategic Snack Summons*, *Ice Coffee Boost*.
1. **Abba's Turn (2 Actions):**
   - *Action 1 (Move):* Walks 1 room right into Blocked *The Salon*.
   - *Action 2 (Tidy):* Uses **Solo Cleanup** to flip *The Salon* straight back to Clear alone!
   - *Event:* Flips *Sticky Counter Situation* (Kitchen) → *The Fridge* is the 1st Cluttered Kitchen, so it flips **Blocked**! *(Good thing Mommy isn't trapped — she can walk right out!)*
2. **Nael's Turn (2 Actions):**
   - *Action 1 (Use Tool):* Spends *Strategic Snack Summons* from the Toolbox to summon Abba into Nael's Cluttered *Dining Table*!
   - *Action 2 (Team Chore):* Because Nael + Abba are in a Cluttered Table room together, **Learning Tower Power!** lets them finish *Set the Shabbat Table* in the same turn! *(Refill Chore Row back to 4 immediately!)*
   - *Event:* Flips *Cousin Aviel Drops By!* → added face-up to the Family Toolbox!
3. **Mommy's Turn (2 Actions + 1 Free Errand):**
   - *Action 1 (Move):* Walks 1 room up out of Blocked *The Fridge* into Clear *Chairs* (Mid-Right, Table).
   - *Free Errand (**Errand Express**):* Delivers *Challah: Mixer to Oven* at *Chairs* for **0 Actions**!
   - *Action 2 (Use Tool):* Plays *Cousin Aviel Drops By!* onto the next face-down Clock card to shield sunset!
   - *Event:* Flips *Fresh Toy Dump!* (Downstairs) → no Downstairs room is Cluttered, so the 1st Clear Downstairs room (*The Salon*) tilts sideways (Cluttered).
4. **Mekimi's Turn (2 Actions):**
   - *Action 1 (Move — **Turbo Crawl**):* Crawls straight across the house into *The Giant Island* (Clear Kitchen)!
   - *Action 2 (Pick Up Errand):* Picks up *Half Baked Harvest Showstopper* (Kitchen → Table) onto her Helper card! *(Refill Chore Row back to 4!)*
   - *Event:* Flips *Out of Shabbat Candles!* (Table) → targets Cluttered *The Dining Table*, where **Abba** is standing — **Balagan Barrier** cancels the flip so it stays Cluttered!
- **End of Round (after Mekimi):** The Clock tries to flip — **Cousin Aviel Drops By!** absorbs the flip and returns to the box! Round 3 starts fresh with **Abba**!

## 9. Printable Quick Reference

- **Turn Order:** **Abba → Nael → Mommy → Mekimi** → flip 1 Clock card after Mekimi!
- **Your Turn:** Take **2 Actions** (Move 1 room, Tidy, Do Team Chore / Pick Up / Deliver Errand, or Use Tool) + **Free Handoff** anytime → **Flip 1 Event**.
- **Chore Row:** Always keep **4 Chores** face-up (refill immediately from Chore Deck!).
- **Slow-Downs (Cluttered-First):** Scan matching zone left-to-right, top-to-bottom (skip Blocked): **1st Cluttered → Blocked; else 1st Clear → Cluttered.**
- **Helpers:**
  - **Abba:** Unblocks Red rooms alone; his Cluttered room can't flip Red.
  - **Mommy:** 1 Free Pick Up/Deliver per turn; Pick Up/Deliver work in Cluttered rooms.
  - **Nael:** With a teammate in a Cluttered room, either may finish a Team Chore there.
  - **Mekimi:** Crawls to **ANY room** on any Move action!

## 10. 1-Page Kid's First-Game Guide (Ages 6+)

### How We Win
Finish **all the Chores** together before **Shabbat Begins!** flips over!

### On Your Turn: Pick 2 Fun Actions, Then Flip 1 Event!
1. **Pick Any 2 Actions** *(you can even do the same one twice!)*:
   - **Walk** 1 room up, down, left, or right. *(Mekimi can crawl to ANY room!)*
   - **Tidy** a sideways room (alone) or a Red room (with a friend — or Abba alone!).
   - **Do a Chore** in a matching Clear room:
     - **Team Chore:** Finish together with a friend in your room!
     - **Errand:** Pick it up at Start, carry it, and drop it off at End! *(Handing Errands to a friend in your room is FREE!)*
   - **Use a Tool:** *Ice Coffee* (clear 1–2 sideways rooms), *Snack Summons* (call a friend straight to you!), or *Cousin Aviel* (block the next Clock flip!).
2. **Flip 1 Event Card:**
   - **Tool:** Yay! Put it in the Toolbox!
   - **Slow-Down:** Find the matching color — if one is sideways, flip it Red! Otherwise turn the first Clear one sideways!
3. **After Mekimi's Turn:** Flip 1 Clock card closer to sunset, then start again with **Abba**!

### Your 4 Family Superpowers!
- **Abba:** Super strong! Fixes Red rooms alone and keeps his sideways room from flipping Red!
- **Mommy:** Errand superstar! Gets 1 FREE Pick Up or Drop Off every turn, even in sideways rooms!
- **Nael:** Learning Tower helper! With a friend in a sideways room, you can still finish a Team Chore together!
- **Mekimi:** Turbo crawler! Can zoom straight into **ANY room** in the whole house!
