# Preparing for Shabbat — Cooper Family Edition

*Official Family Rulebook · v3 Simplified Edition · 1–4 Players · Ages 6+ · 15–20 Minutes*

Components: 72 poker-size cards + 4 3D-Printed Family Helpers (Abba, Mommy, Nael, Mekimi) | Play Online: https://joshcooper417.github.io/Shabbat-game/


---

## 1. Goal & Key Basics

Work together as a family to finish all Game Chores before Shabbat Begins! flips face-up on the Shabbat Clock.

> Win Right Away: The instant the final Chore enters the Done Pile (Chore Row, Chore Deck, and all carried Errands are empty), Shabbat Shalom—you win! Skip the Event flip and Clock flip.
> Lose Right Away: If Shabbat Begins! flips face-up before all Chores are done, stop right away and try again!


### Key Basics

- 3D Helpers vs. Helper Cards: Your 4 3D-Printed Family Helpers move on top of the 3×3 Room cards. Your 4 Helper Power Cards stay face-up in front of players to show powers and hold carried Errands. (Any number of Helpers may share a room!)
- Room States: Every Room card is Clear (upright), Cluttered (sideways), or Blocked (flipped to its shaded red side—the zone icon stays visible).
- Next-Door Room: Straight up, down, left, or right (not diagonal).
- Matching Room: Match the color and icon! Any orange Kitchen Chore works on any orange Kitchen room.
- Clock Flip: Flip the leftmost face-down Clock card face-up.
- Table Talk: Discuss freely! The player controlling the active Helper makes the final call.

## 2. What's in the Box (72 Cards)

Everything fits in 72 poker-size cards plus your 4 3D-Printed Family Helpers—no dice, tokens, or scorepads needed!

- Rooms & Helpers (16 cards): 12 Double-Sided Room Cards — 3 each of Kitchen (orange), Downstairs Balagan (teal), Table (blue), and Upstairs (purple), with a Clear front and red Blocked back — plus 4 Helper Power Cards: Abba, Mommy, Nael, and Mekimi.
- Chores Deck (18 cards): 10 Team Chores (finish together with 2+ Helpers in a matching Clear room) and 8 Errands (shows Start → End; pick up at Start, carry on your Helper card, and deliver to End).
- Shabbat Clock (10 cards): 9 Friday Afternoon + 1 Shabbat Begins!
- Events & Tools Deck (28 cards, shared back): 18 Slow-Downs (15 Single-Zone, 2 Double Trouble, and 1 Time Flies: “Wait—Shabbat Is at WHAT Time?!”) + 10 Household Tools (4 Ice Coffee Boost, 3 Strategic Snack Summons, and 3 Cousin Aviel Drops By!).

## 3. Setup (Standard Mode)

First game? Check Section 10 for Beginner Mode!

1. Build the 3×3 House: Shuffle the 12 Room cards and deal 9 upright (Clear) in a 3×3 grid with a small gap between cards. Make sure all 4 zone colors appear—if one is missing, swap the bottom-right room for a boxed room of that color. Box the 3 unused rooms.
2. Starting Clutter: Turn the Top-Center and Bottom-Center rooms sideways (Cluttered). All 4 corners start Clear!
3. Place the 4 Helpers: Set the 3D Helpers in the 4 corners: Top-Left: Abba · Top-Right: Nael · Bottom-Left: Mekimi · Bottom-Right: Mommy. Put all 4 Helper Power Cards face-up near the players.
4. Deal the Chores: Shuffle all 18 Chores once and deal 13 (box the other 5 unseen). Flip 4 face-up into the Chore Row and stack 9 face-down as the Chore Deck. Leave space for the Done Pile.
5. Build the Shabbat Clock: Place 5 Friday Afternoon cards face-down left to right, with Shabbat Begins! face-down on the far right (6 total; box the other 4).
6. Tools & Event Deck: Shuffle all 10 Tools face-down: put 2 face-up in the Family Toolbox (no limit), shuffle 4 with all 18 Slow-Downs face-down to form the Event Deck (22 cards), and box 4 Tools unseen. Leave room for one Event Discard Pile.

## 4. Players & Turn Order

> Clockwise Round Order (Every Game): Abba → Nael → Mommy → Mekimi


### Assigning Helpers (1–4 Players)

- 4 Players: Each player controls 1 Helper.
- 3 Players: The oldest player (or any volunteer) controls 2 Helpers (for example, Abba & Mekimi); the other two players control 1 Helper each.
- 2 Players: Player 1 controls Abba & Mommy (turns 1 & 3); Player 2 controls Nael & Mekimi (turns 2 & 4) so you alternate every turn!
- 1 Player (Solo): Control all 4 Helpers in order.


### On Every Turn: Just 2 Easy Steps!

- Step 1 — Take Up to 2 Actions (in any order; repeats allowed!): Choose from Move, Tidy, Do a Chore, or Use a Tool. (Handing Errands to a teammate in your room is always FREE!)
- Step 2 — Flip 1 Event Card (skip if you already won!): Then play passes clockwise. After Mekimi's turn, flip 1 Shabbat Clock card and start the next round with Abba!


---

## 5. Actions: Move & Tidy

On your turn, take up to 2 Actions in any order—you may even do the same action twice (like Move → Move or Chore → Chore!).

- Action: Move (1 Action): Walk 1 room straight up, down, left, or right (not diagonal). Cluttered and Blocked rooms never trap you—you can always walk into or out of them normally! Any number of Helpers may stand in the same room. (Mekimi's Turbo Crawl: Whenever Mekimi spends 1 Action to Move, she crawls straight into ANY room on the board!)
- Action: Tidy Room (1 Action): Cluttered → Clear: Stand in a sideways room and turn it upright (1 Helper). Blocked → Clear: Flip a red Blocked room straight back to Clear! Requires 2 or more Helpers in that room—or Abba alone (Solo Cleanup).

> Great Combo: Because you get 2 Actions, you can Tidy a Cluttered room on Action 1 and immediately finish a Chore there on Action 2!

## Room States: CAN & CANNOT

Check the orientation of the Room card your Helper stands on:

| Room State | ✓ You CAN | ✗ You CANNOT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear (upright) | ✓ Everything! Move in/out, Team Chores, Pick Up/Deliver/Handoff Errands, and Tools. | Nothing—everything works here! |
| Cluttered (sideways) | ✓ Move in/out, Tidy (1 Helper), Free Handoff, Tools. Team Chores if Nael + teammate are here. Errands on Mommy's turn. | ✗ Team Chores (without Nael). ✗ Pick Up or Deliver Errands (if not Mommy). |
| Blocked (red back) | ✓ Move in/out, Unblock (2+ Helpers or Abba alone), Free Handoff, Tools. | ✗ No Team Chores. ✗ No Pick Up or Deliver Errands (not even Nael or Mommy!). |

> Watch Out for Cluttered Rooms! Slow-Downs always flip Cluttered rooms to Red Blocked first—Tidy messy rooms before they flip red!


---

## 6. Actions: Chores & Errands

There are only 2 Chore types—both match the room's color and icon:

- Team Chores (10 cards · Purple): With 2 or more Helpers in a matching Clear room (or Cluttered if Nael is here!), spend 1 Action to move the card straight to the Done Pile!
- Errands (8 cards · Blue Start → End): Pick Up (1 Action): In a matching Clear Start room, place the Errand on your Helper card (no carry limit!). Deliver (1 Action): In a matching Clear End room, move 1 carried Errand to the Done Pile! Mommy's Errand Express: Once per turn, Mommy Picks Up or Delivers for FREE (even with 0 Actions left!), and works in Cluttered rooms too!
- Free Handoff (0 Actions — Any Room!): Anytime on your turn, freely give or take any carried Errand(s) with a teammate standing in your room! (Carried Errands can't be dropped back to the Chore Row.)
- Instant Chore Row Refill: Whenever a Chore leaves the Chore Row (finished or picked up), immediately flip the top card of the Chore Deck so 4 Chores stay face-up!


---

## 7. Actions: Household Tools

Spend 1 Action from ANY room to play a face-up Tool from the Family Toolbox (there is no cap on how many Tools can sit in the Toolbox!):

- Ice Coffee Boost (4 cards · Reusable): Turn 1 or 2 Cluttered rooms anywhere on the board back to Clear (cannot fix Blocked rooms), then place in the Event Discard Pile.
- Strategic Snack Summons (3 cards · Reusable): Pull any 1 teammate from anywhere on the board straight into your room (their carried Errands come with them!), then place in the Event Discard Pile.
- Cousin Aviel Drops By! (3 cards · One-Time Shield): Place face-up on the leftmost unshielded face-down Clock card. When that card would flip (from Round End or Time Flies), return Cousin Aviel to the box instead! (Multiple shields may guard consecutive Clock cards.)

> One Shared Event Discard: Spent Reusable Tools go into the same Event Discard Pile as Slow-Downs—so when the Event Deck reshuffles, your Tools naturally come back!


---

## 8. Events & End of Round

After your 2 Actions (skip if you already won!), flip the top card of the Event Deck. If the deck is empty, shuffle the entire Event Discard Pile face-down first.

- Household Tool: Place it face-up in the Family Toolbox!
- Slow-Down (Universal Cluttered-First Rule): Scan rooms matching the zone icon left to right, top to bottom (skip Blocked rooms): (1) If any matching room is Cluttered, flip the 1st Cluttered room to Red Blocked! (Canceled if Abba is standing there!) (2) Else if any matching room is Clear, turn the 1st Clear room sideways (Cluttered)! (3) If all matching rooms are already Blocked, nothing happens. Then place the Slow-Down in the Event Discard Pile.
- Double Trouble: Resolve the left zone icon first, then the right zone icon with the exact same rule.
- Time Flies (“Wait—Shabbat Is at WHAT Time?!”): (1) Trigger 1 Clock flip first (Shabbat Begins! = lose right away, unless Cousin Aviel shields it!); (2) hit Kitchen using the same rule; (3) return this card to the box.

> End of Round (Only After Mekimi's Turn!): Flip the leftmost face-down Clock card (or box a Cousin Aviel shield on it). If Shabbat Begins! flips, you lose! Otherwise, start the next round with Abba.

## 11. Quick 1-Round Example

A sample Standard-mode round showing combos and all 4 Cooper Helpers:

- 1. Abba (2 Actions): Walks right into Red Blocked The Salon (Action 1) and uses Solo Cleanup to flip it Clear alone (Action 2)! Event: Kitchen Slow-Down → Cluttered The Fridge (where Mommy stands) flips Blocked. Mommy isn't trapped!
- 2. Nael (2 Actions): Plays Snack Summons to pull Abba into Nael's Cluttered Dining Table (Action 1), then uses Learning Tower Power! to finish Set the Shabbat Table right away (Action 2)! Refill Row to 4. Event: Draws Cousin Aviel Drops By! → added to Family Toolbox!
- 3. Mommy (2 Actions + Free Errand): Walks out of Blocked Fridge into Clear Chairs (Action 1), Delivers Challah for FREE (Errand Express!), and puts Cousin Aviel on the Clock (Action 2)! Event: Downstairs Slow-Down → Clear Salon tilts sideways.

Event: Table Slow-Down targets Cluttered Dining Table—Abba's Balagan Barrier cancels the flip! End of Round: Cousin Aviel blocks the Clock flip and returns to the box! Round 3 starts with Abba!

## Kid's First-Game Guide (6+)

> How We Win: Finish all the Chores together before Shabbat Begins! flips over!

1. Pick Any 2 Fun Actions (you can even do the same one twice!): Walk 1 room up, down, left, or right (Mekimi crawls ANYWHERE!) · Tidy a sideways room (alone) or a Red room (with a friend—or Abba alone!) · Team Chore (Purple): Meet a friend in a matching Clear room and finish it! · Errand (Blue): Pick up at Start, carry it, drop off at End! (Handing to a friend in your room is FREE!) · Use a Tool from the Toolbox (from any room)!
2. Flip 1 Event Card: Tool → Toolbox! Slow-Down → match the color: flip the 1st sideways room Red (or tilt the 1st Clear room sideways!).
3. After Mekimi: Flip 1 Clock card closer to sunset, then go back to Abba!

> Superpowers: Abba fixes Red rooms alone & keeps his sideways room from flipping Red · Nael finishes Team Chores in sideways rooms with a friend · Mommy gets 1 FREE Errand/turn (even sideways!) · Mekimi crawls to ANY room!

## Tabletop Quick Reference

> Turn Order: Abba → Nael → Mommy → Mekimi → Clock Flip! | Every Turn: 2 Actions (+ Free Handoff) → Flip 1 Event

- 4 Actions (Pick Any 2): Move (1 room straight) · Tidy (Cluttered: 1 / Red: 2 or Abba) · Chore (Team 2+ or Errand) · Tool (Play from Toolbox).
- Slow-Downs (1 Rule!): Scan matching zone L→R, T→B: 1st Cluttered → Blocked! Else 1st Clear → Cluttered!
- Room States Cheat Sheet (Keep Face-Up on Table!): Clear (Upright): Everything works! Team Chores, Errands, Handoffs, Tools. · Cluttered (Tilted): Walk, Tidy (1), Handoff, Tools. (Team w/ Nael; Errands w/ Mommy.) · Red Blocked (“Stuck”): Walk, Handoff, Tools only. Fix w/ 2+ Helpers or Abba!
- 4 Family Superpowers: Abba: Unblocks Red alone; his Cluttered room can't flip Red (canceled!). · Nael: With teammate in Cluttered room, either finishes Team Chores (any turn). · Mommy: 1 FREE Errand/turn (even at 0 Actions!); Errands work in Cluttered. · Mekimi: Crawls straight into ANY room on any Move!
- Modes: Beg 10 Chores / 7 Clock (no Time Flies) · Std 13 / 6 · Hard 15 / 6
