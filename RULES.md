# Preparing for Shabbat — Cooper Family Edition

**Official Family Rulebook** *(Transcribed from the latest 16-page Small Booklet & 72-card TGC Print Pack)*  
**1–4 Players · Ages 6+ · 15–20 Minutes**

---

## 1. Goal & Key Terms

Work together to finish all **Game Chores** (**9** Standard; **7** Beginner; **10** Hard) before **Shabbat Begins!** flips face-up.

- **Win Right Away:** When every Chore is in the **Done Pile** (Arrival Pile empty; none in the Chore Row, tucked under rooms, or carried). **Skip Step 3 & any Clock flip!**
- **Lose Right Away:** If **Shabbat Begins!** flips first — stop right away (skip any remaining Event text and do not Rest).

### Key Terms
- **Helpers vs. Cards:** 3D Helpers move on the 3×3 Rooms; Helper Power cards stay in front of players for Ready/Resting & carried Errands.
- **Room States:** **Clear** (upright), **Cluttered** (sideways), or **Blocked** (red side).
- **Helper States:** **Ready** (upright) or **Resting** (sideways — already went this round, still helps!). **Teammate** = any other Helper (even yours in 1–2P).
- **Next-Door Room:** Up, down, left, or right only — **never diagonal** (Corner = 2, Edge = 3, Center = 4).
- **Table Talk:** Discuss freely! The active player (or Mekimi's guide in 3P) makes the final call, including Backup Tools.

## 3. Setup (Steps A–G)

*Standard setup shown below (see Section 7 for Beginner & Hard counts).*

- **A. Build the 3×3 House:** Shuffle all 12 Rooms; deal **9 Clear** in a 3×3 grid with ~3–4 cm gaps so cards turn freely. Ensure all 4 zone icons appear (if missing one, swap the bottom-right card; a 3/3/2/1 split is fine). Box the 3 unused Rooms.
- **B. Place the 4 Helpers:** Set near a corner/edge of each corner card (max **2 Helpers** per room):
  - **Top-Left:** Abba · **Top-Right:** Nael · **Bottom-Right:** Mommy · **Bottom-Left:** Mekimi.
- **C. Starting Clutter:** Shuffle **7 Friday Afternoon + 2 Chore** cards (*Beginner: 8+1; Hard: 7+2*); deal 1 per room and flip. Turn rooms with Chore cards sideways (**Cluttered**). Helpers may start in Cluttered rooms, and **Always On** powers apply on Turn 1. Return all 9 cards to their decks!
- **D. Seats & Turn Order** *(all 4 Helpers act once per round)*:
  - **1–2P:** Alternate **Group A** (Abba + Nael) & **Group B** (Mekimi + Mommy); pick whichever Ready Helper in your group you want.
  - **3P:** Abba, Nael, Mommy clockwise; **Mekimi** acts 4th (extra turn for that round's guide — youngest in Round 1, or born most recently if tied; rotates clockwise each round).
  - **4P:** Clockwise: **Abba → Nael → Mommy → Mekimi**.
- **E. Deal Chores:** Sort Chores by the 4 types; draw **1 of each** (4). Shuffle the other 14, draw **5 more** (*Beginner: 3; Hard: 6*), and box the rest unseen. Shuffle your **9 Chores** (*Beginner: 7; Hard: 10*): place **5 face-up** (*Beginner: 3; Hard: 6*) in the **Chore Row** (no max size) and **4 face-down** in the **Arrival Pile**.
- **F. Build Shabbat Clock:** Place **7 Friday Afternoon** cards (*Beginner: 8; Hard: 5*) face-down in a row, with **Shabbat Begins!** face-down at the **far right**. Box extra Clock cards.
- **G. Tools & Event Deck:** Shuffle all 10 Tools:
  - **2 face-up** in the **Ready Tool Row** (max 3).
  - **2 face-up** in **Backup Tools** (doesn't count toward the 3-card Ready limit).
  - Shuffle **2 Tools + 15 Slow-Downs** (*Beginner: 14; Hard: 16*) to make the **Event Deck**. Box the other 4 Tools & unused Slow-Downs unseen *(some games have fewer or no Early Prep cards)*.

> **Two Separate Discards:** Keep **Slow-Down Discard** (reshuffles when Event Deck empties) separate from **Tool Discard** (for Recharging only — never shuffled into Events).

## 4. Turn Order & Turn Steps

**`1. Move (Optional)` → `2. Do 1 Action (Optional)` → `3. Flip 1 Event & Rest`**

Each round starts with **Abba** (3–4P) or **Group A** (1–2P), then goes clockwise.

### Step 1: Move (Optional)
Stay put, OR move into any room with **fewer than 2 Helpers** *(any Move may leave a Cluttered or Blocked room)*:
- **Hallway Slide:** Move **1 or 2 rooms straight** (up, down, left, right).
  - You *may* pass through a full **Clear** room (2 Helpers).
  - You **must stop** in the 1st **Cluttered** or **Blocked** room you enter (can't enter if it already has 2 Helpers).
- **Buddy-Hop:** Jump straight into *any* room holding **1 teammate** (Ready or Resting, even Cluttered or Blocked!).
- **Turbo Crawl (Mekimi only):** Move **1 room diagonally** into any room with fewer than 2 Helpers.

> **Resting Teammates Still Help!** They count for Room Limit, Buddy-Hop, Tidy bonus, fixing Blocked rooms, Room Relay, Team Combos, Nael's & Abba's powers, Pass, and Recharge — and helping **never** makes your own card Rest.

### Step 2: Do 1 Action (Optional)
Choose **1 action** that changes the board (e.g., can't Tidy a Clear room), OR **skip Step 2 freely** (never forced to spend a Tool or Pass).

| Room State | Allowed Actions | How to Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clear (↑)** | Everything! | Already Clear |
| **Cluttered (Sideways)** | Pass & Tools; tucked Chore & Combo w/ **Nael + 1**; Pick Up/Deliver Errands on **Mommy's turn** | Tidy (1 Helper) or Ice Coffee |
| **Blocked (Red)** | Pass, Tools, Move out only *(no Chores/Errands — not even Nael or Mommy!)* | Tidy w/ **2 Helpers** or **Abba alone** |

#### Action 1: Tidy Room
- **Cluttered → Clear.** With a teammate in your room, you may also turn **up to 1** next-door Cluttered room Clear.
- **Blocked → Clear** (needs **2 Helpers**, or **Abba alone**; no next-door bonus).

#### Action 2: Do Chore
- **Start Room Chore (Clear):** Tuck 1 matching Room Chore under your room (max 1 per room).
- **Finish Tucked Chore (Room Relay):** Move to Done if a teammate stands in your **row, column, or room** *(no diagonals; Cluttered/Blocked rooms in between & teammate's room/card state don't matter)*. Works in Clear rooms, or Cluttered rooms with **Nael + 1**.
- **Restore Order (Clear):** Finish when your room AND every next-door room are Clear at that moment.

#### Action 3: Run Errand
- **Pick Up (Clear Start room — or Cluttered on Mommy's turn):** Place 1 matching Errand under your Helper card *(Carry limit: **1** Abba/Nael; **2** Mommy/Mekimi)*.
- **Deliver (Clear End room — or Cluttered on Mommy's turn):** Move **1 carried Errand** (1 action = 1 Errand) to Done → **Bonus: Return 1 used Tool** from Tool Discard to Ready if under 3 *(still finishes if Ready is full or Discard is empty)*.
- **Pass (Any room):** Hand 1 carried Errand to a teammate in your room (within their carry limit). Carried Errands can't be dropped back to Chore Row and stay with a Helper moved by *Strategic Snack Summons*.

#### Action 4: Team Combo
With a teammate in **your room** (Clear, or Cluttered with **Nael + 1**), choose one:
- **Team Pair:** Finish 1 matching Team Pair → **Bonus: Return 1 used Tool** from Tool Discard to Ready if under 3 *(still finishes if full/empty)*.
- **1-Step Room Chore:** Finish 1 matching Room Chore straight from Chore Row or tucked under your room *(no Tool bonus)*.

> **Empty Chore Row?** Whenever the Chore Row hits **0 cards** (for any reason), **immediately** flip 1 Arrival Chore face-up — in addition to the End-of-Round flip!

#### Action 5: Use OR Recharge a Tool (Any Room)
Choose **one** (never both). Tools are played **only** as your Step 2 action from the Ready Tool Row — never out of turn or in reaction to an Event:
- **Ice Coffee Boost (Reusable):** Turn **1 or 2 Cluttered rooms** anywhere Clear (requires at least 1 Cluttered room; can't fix Blocked), then put in Tool Discard.
- **Strategic Snack Summons (Reusable):** Move **any teammate** from anywhere into your room (if under 2 Helpers here), then put in Tool Discard.
- **Early Prep Paid Off! (One-Time Shield):** Place on next face-down Clock card *(works even on Shabbat Begins!; frees its Ready slot immediately; max 1 shield on Clock at a time)*. When it blocks a flip, **box it forever**. *(If sent to Tool Discard as overflow before shielding, it CAN be Recharged!)*
- **Recharge Action (With a Teammate):** Return **1 used Tool** of choice from Tool Discard to Ready (if under 3). **Mekimi's Big Smile Reset** returns **up to 2** *(Recharge action only!)*.

### Step 3: Events & Round End
*(Skip Step 3 completely if you already finished the last Chore in Step 2!)*

1. **Flip 1 Event Card:**
   - **Household Tool:** Add to Ready Tool Row (if under 3); otherwise put in Tool Discard. Do not draw another card.
   - **Slow-Down:** Resolve it (Section 5), then put in Slow-Down Discard — except *"Wait—Shabbat Is at WHAT Time?!"* which always returns to the **box**.
   - **Empty Event Deck?** Shuffle **only Slow-Down Discard** to form a new Event Deck. *(If both Event Deck and Slow-Down Discard are empty, skip the flip; still Rest.)*
2. **Rest Your Helper:** Turn your Helper card sideways (**Resting**). Carried Errands stay underneath.
3. **End of Round (All 4 Helpers Resting):**
   - **Clock Flip:** If *Early Prep Paid Off!* is on the next Clock card, box Early Prep instead of flipping. Otherwise flip the leftmost Clock card (**Shabbat Begins!** = lose!).
   - **Refresh:** Stand all 4 Helper cards upright (**Ready**).
   - **Arrival Chore:** Flip 1 Arrival Chore into the Chore Row (if any remain).

## 5. Slow-Down Targeting & Red Blocked Rooms

### How to Pick the Room (`1st` / `2nd` / `3rd`)
- **Skip Blocked rooms.** Scan all remaining Clear/Cluttered rooms of that zone **left-to-right, top-to-bottom**, row by row. *(Abba's room still counts unless already Blocked.)*
- **Exact Number First:** Always pick that numbered room — even if Cluttered! Only if fewer non-Blocked rooms of that zone exist, fall back to the **1st Clear** (else **1st Cluttered**).
- **Double Trouble (2 icons):** Resolve left icon, then right icon. For each, pick the **1st Clear** room (else **1st Cluttered**).
- **Time Flies (*"Wait—Shabbat Is at WHAT Time?!"*):**
  1. Flip next Clock card **first** (skip round-end Refresh/Arrival!). If *Early Prep* absorbs it, box Early Prep now — End-of-Round flip later is **not** absorbed unless a new one is played. If *Shabbat Begins!* flips, lose right away (skip steps 2–3 & don't Rest).
  2. Hit **1st Clear Kitchen** (else **1st Cluttered**).
  3. **Always** box this card (even if all Kitchens are Blocked).
- **All Blocked?** If every room of that zone is Blocked, that room effect is lost harmlessly.

### Everything About Blocked Rooms
- **How It Happens:** A Slow-Down hits a **Clear** room → **Cluttered** (tucked Chores stay tucked). A Slow-Down hits a **Cluttered** room → flip it to its **red Blocked** side.
- **Abba's Balagan Barrier:** While Abba stands in a Cluttered room (Ready or Resting), a Slow-Down **can't** flip it Blocked! The flip is canceled (no redirect, no Backup Tool unlocked).
- **When a Room Flips Blocked:**
  1. Return any tucked Chore face-up to the **Chore Row**.
  2. Helpers & carried Errands stay safe (can move out, Pass, use Tools, or Tidy).
  3. **Unlock 1 Backup Tool:** If Ready Tool Row has under 3 Tools, active player moves **1 Tool** from **Backup Tools** to Ready *(if 3/3 full, Backup Tools stay put; they never refill)*.
- **How to Fix:** Use **Tidy Room** with **2 Helpers** — OR **Abba alone** (*Solo Cleanup*). Flips straight back to Clear! *(Ice Coffee can't fix Blocked; no next-door bonus.)*

## 6. Meet the 4 Helpers

- **ABBA** *(Top-Left · Carry: 1 Errand)*
  - **Solo Cleanup (Your Turn):** Flip a Blocked room you're in back to Clear alone.
  - **Balagan Barrier (Always On):** Cluttered room with Abba can't flip Blocked.
- **MOMMY** *(Bottom-Right · Carry: 2 Errands)*
  - **Errand Express (Your Turn):** On Mommy's own turn, Pick Up or Deliver Errands even in a **Cluttered** room (not Blocked; works alone or with a teammate).
- **NAEL** *(Top-Right · Carry: 1 Errand)*
  - **Learning Tower Power! (Always On):** When Nael and a teammate share a Cluttered room, either may finish a tucked Chore or do a Team Combo.
- **MEKIMI** *(Bottom-Left · Carry: 2 Errands · Your Turn)*
  - **Turbo Crawl:** Move 1 room diagonally in Step 1.
  - **Big Smile Reset:** On a Recharge action with a teammate, return **up to 2 used Tools** to Ready (max 3 Ready).

### Family Winning Tips
- Empty the Chore Row early to reveal Arrival Chores right away!
- Shield the Clock with *Early Prep Paid Off!* (+1 round each!).
- Use Mommy for Cluttered Errands and Nael for Cluttered Chores!
